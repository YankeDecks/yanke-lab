Ohio's turn.

HB 564 would require private insurers to cover medically necessary prosthetic and orthotic devices, including devices for physical activity.

Method and sources

Ohio was not one of the 23 states in the July 2026 multistate study, so this tool applies that study's published method to Ohio's Census and KFF figures. It is a policy estimate, not an actuarial certification.

Per-device costs come from Minnesota's 2024 actuarial evaluation ($0.39 PMPM in its first year). Activity-specific devices add 30% on top of general-use prosthetics. The result is adjusted for Ohio's lower insured share and higher disability rate. State mandates reach fully insured plans only, so cost and savings are both overstated by the same proportion.
